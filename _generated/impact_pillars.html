We are in the middle of a biodiversity crisis. The IPBES estimates one million species face extinction within decades — yet the tools to predict where, how fast, and what we can do about it have historically sat behind high computing barriers, specialist software, and institutional gatekeepers. My work is about closing that gap.

Ecological Intelligence at National Scale

Through EcoCommons, I help make cloud-based species distribution and biodiversity modelling accessible to ecologists across Australia — without requiring institutional computing infrastructure or deep technical expertise. Every reproducible workflow published is a method that can be reused, audited, and built upon by the whole community.

From Data to Conservation Decision

My position between technical development and the scientific community is the bridge. I support the full journey from raw data to actionable insight — testing digital products for usability, training ecologists in new workflows, translating findings across disciplines, and communicating complex ecological information to the people who need it most. Adapting to new tools, discovering better solutions, and connecting science to real-world problems is where I work best.

Science That Reaches People

The most rigorous analysis means nothing if it stays locked in a specialist pipeline. I prioritise connecting ecological solutions to the communities, organisations, and decision-makers who need them — whether that's a regional NGO, a government agency, or an international research body. Open platforms, plain-language outputs, and direct engagement are how science earns its impact.
